# GTU Diploma Computer Syllabus Archive

A permanent archive of Gujarat Technological University (GTU) Diploma in Engineering, Computer Engineering (branch 07) teaching schemes: every subject code, subject name, category, lecture/tutorial/practical hours, credits and marks breakup, for every scheme and semester available on the official GTU syllabus portal.

Archived from the official portal (old26.gtu.ac.in/Syllabus/Syllabus.aspx) starting 2026-11-13, so the record survives even if the portal changes or removes old schemes.

Notes on the portal data: the 2011 scheme's Semester 1-2 lists are the common first-year pool shared across all diploma branches, exactly as the portal returns them for branch 07. The new DI scheme's Semester 6 is not yet published on the portal. Some diploma academic-year selections return no rows on the portal even though the subjects exist in the unfiltered lists (for this branch, 2013-14 returned no Semester 4-6 rows), so this archive is built from both year-anchored and unfiltered portal searches.

## PDF backup

Offline copies of the syllabus PDFs are kept in a Google Drive folder, organised by scheme: [GTU Diploma Computer Syllabus PDFs](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1FrTiJSzsBwh59inZDvm1reqdcfOh2gyC). 269 PDF files are archived there, covering 271 of the 294 subject entries (two codes, 310049 and 320016, repeat across semesters and share one PDF). 23 codes have no PDF on GTU's server, so for those only the scheme data in this archive is preserved: the 15 MOOC courses (imb26-mg246, ini26-cs07, noc26-cs131, noc26-cs143, noc26-cs144, noc26-cs150, noc26-cs153, noc26-cs182, noc26-cs99, noc26-hs252, noc26-mg114, nou26-cs21, nou26-ge87, ntr26-ed149, ntr26-ed169), 5 subjects of the 2011 scheme (310001, 310014, 310047, 320024, 350705), the two 2012-scheme project subjects (3350706, 3360707), and DI01000151 of the DI scheme.

## Columns

L = Lectures, T = Tutorial, P = Practical, PBL = Project Based Learning, E = External theory marks, M = Internal assessment, I = Internal viva/submission, V = External viva, Total = total marks. Values are exactly as shown on the GTU portal.
